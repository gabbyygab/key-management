How the split works

Leave Group blank for the whole block, e.g. BSIT-4D. Add G1 or G2 to file a group, e.g. BSIT-4DG1.

The whole block and its groups are the same students, so a block-wide class blocks both groups. Two groups may still meet at the same time in different rooms.
