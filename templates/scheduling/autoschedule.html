What the run will not do

  • Move a class that is already placed — an earlier offering keeps what it took.
  • Double-book a room, a professor, or a section (a block-wide class blocks both of its groups).
  • Touch a hand-made booking, even on a rebuild.

An offering that will not fit comes back below with the reason. Fix it with Swap on the timetable, or by unscheduling whatever is in the way.
